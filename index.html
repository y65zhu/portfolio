Hello

I'm Annora — a marketer who listens first.

I grew up in my family's restaurant in Beijing, and I've been behind some kind of counter ever since. A restaurant, a dessert shop, a nail salon, a beauty clinic, an arcade, an e-commerce platform, an education company, a property management office — eight industries, eight kinds of customers, one lesson: people rarely say what they really want, but they'll tell you if you're honest with them.

Now I turn that into brands, campaigns and products at the University of Waterloo.
