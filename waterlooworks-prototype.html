Interactive prototype · WaterlooWorks · UX redesign

A fairer job posting, one step at a time.

A full redesign of the employer side of WaterlooWorks: a guided, multi-step form with transparent evaluation criteria, fair-hiring prompts and a live preview of what students will see. Fill it in the way an employer would — everything below is clickable.

Prototype · Create job postingOpen full screen ↗

Best on a laptop or desktop — it's an employer tool, built at desktop size and scaled to fit here.

What to try

Four steps, each one making the posting more honest.

1 · Job basics

The essentials up front, with a fair-hiring tip beside the form instead of buried in a policy page.

2 · Role description

Write the role while a student-view preview updates next to it — employers see their posting the way applicants will.

3 · Evaluation criteria

Weight each criterion with sliders that have to add up to 100%, so students know exactly how they'll be judged — and GPA isn't the only thing that counts.

4 · Interview process

Set the interview steps and commit to giving feedback to candidates who don't get the role.
